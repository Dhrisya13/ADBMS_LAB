2. Find the number of customers in each city, and only include cities with more than 2 customers.

mysql> SELECT city, COUNT(*) AS total_customer FROM CUSTOMER GROUP BY city HAVING COUNT(*)>=2;

| city    | total_customer |
+---------+----------------+
| calicut |              2 |
| plkd    |              2 |
