1. List the product names, only include those purchased quantity more than 3.
2. Find the product that has no sale.
3. List the names of all customers who have purchased at least 2 products, making sure that
no duplicate names are in the result.

1.mysql> SELECT p_name FROM PRODUCT WHERE P_id IN (SELECT P_id FROM Invoice_Item WHERE quantity<3);
+---------+
| p_name  |
+---------+
| Apple   |
| Samsung |
| Oppo    |
| Vivo    |
+---------+
4 rows in set (0.00 sec)

2.mysql> SELECT p_name FROM PRODUCT WHERE P_id NOT IN (SELECT P_id FROM Invoice_Item );
+--------+
| p_name |
+--------+
| Realme |
| Moto   |
+--------+
2 rows in set (0.00 sec)

3.mysql> SELECT name FROM CUSTOMER WHERE cust_id IN (SELECT cust_id FROM Invoice_master WHERE inv_id IN (SELECT inv_id FROM Invoice_Item GROUP BY
inv_id HAVING COUNT(P_id)<2));
